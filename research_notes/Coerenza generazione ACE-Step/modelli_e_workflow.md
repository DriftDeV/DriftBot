## 1. DiT checkpoint and LM size: quality differences, recommendations, VRAM

### Takeaway
Officially, turbo (8 steps, no CFG) is the default and rated "Very High" quality; sft (50 steps, CFG) is rated "High" with richer detail; base is rated "Medium" quality but "High" diversity and is the only model with extract/lego/complete. Nobody (docs, paper, HF card) publishes a controlled long-form coherence comparison between them. The LM (planner) is the only documented structural-coherence mechanism; 4B is rated "Strong" at composition/copy-melody, 0.6B "Medium"/"Weak". On 8 GB only 2B DiT (turbo/sft/base) + 0.6B LM is supported; XL (4B DiT) is not.

### Cited Findings
- Model table (DOCUMENTED): base = pretrain only, CFG, 50 steps, Quality Medium, Diversity High, Fine-tunability Easy; sft = pretrain+SFT, CFG, 50 steps, Quality High, Diversity Medium; turbo = pretrain+SFT+distill, no CFG, 8 steps, Quality Very High, Diversity Medium; all support reference audio, text2music, cover, repaint; extract/lego/complete only for base — [README.md Model Zoo](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5) (local `README.md` lines 256-262) and [HF card](https://huggingface.co/ACE-Step/Ace-Step1.5). The HF card also lists `acestep-v15-turbo-rl` as "To be released".
- Turbo variants differ only in the shift used during distillation: `turbo` (default; joint shift 1,2,3; "best balance of creativity and semantics, thoroughly tested"), `turbo-shift1` (richer detail, weaker semantics), `turbo-shift3` (clearer, richer timbre, may sound "dry", minimal orchestration), `turbo-continuous` (experimental, shift 1-5, not thoroughly tested). Larger shift = more effort on early denoising = "stronger semantics, clearer overall framework"; smaller = more detail but possibly noise — `docs/en/Tutorial.md` lines 195-212. API example names `acestep-v15-turbo-shift3` as a selectable `model` — `docs/en/API.md` line ~158.
- SFT vs turbo (DOCUMENTED): sft has CFG and 50 steps; "more steps mean error accumulation, audio clarity may be slightly inferior to Turbo. But its detail expression and semantic parsing will be better"; LM codes also work with sft — `docs/en/Tutorial.md` lines 214-222.
- Base: "strongest plasticity", recommended for large-scale fine-tuning — `docs/en/Tutorial.md` lines 224-234. Guide recommends for best quality: base, steps 64+, `use_adg=True`, `guidance_scale` 7-9, `shift=3.0`, lossless output — `docs/en/INFERENCE.md` lines ~1010-1017. API: `shift` and `guidance_scale` "only effective for base models" — `docs/en/API.md` lines ~192, 217.
- Official workflow hint: "Most people use Turbo for day-to-day work and switch to SFT/Base for final versions" — [Musician's Guide](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5/blob/main/docs/en/ace_step_musicians_guide.md) line ~524.
- Combination table: fastest = turbo + no LM/0.6B; daily = turbo + 1.7B (default); detail = sft + 1.7B or 4B; special tasks/fine-tuning = base — `docs/en/Tutorial.md` lines 278-287.
- LM sizes (DOCUMENTED): identical training data, difference is capacity; larger = more world knowledge, "stronger memory (e.g., remembering reference audio melodies)", better on long-tail styles. Table: 0.6B Basic/Weak, 1.7B Medium/Medium (default), 4B Rich/Strong — `docs/en/Tutorial.md` lines 166-186. README LM table: Composition Capability Medium/Medium/Strong; Copy Melody Weak/Medium/Strong — `README.md` lines 276-280.
- Planner role: LM emits metadata (BPM, key, duration, structure) as YAML then audio codes; FSQ tokenizer compresses 25 Hz latents to 5 Hz discrete codes (~64k codebook) that act as the "structural source latent"; coherence on SongEval = 4.72 (tied with Suno-v5) using "1.7B LM + dynamic distilled DiT (no RL)"; paper does not name or compare turbo/sft/base and gives no long-form-specific coherence metric — [arXiv 2602.00744](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.00744v3) (as summarized by fetch; full text not read directly). Paper conclusion: capabilities "bounded by the parameter constraints necessary for consumer-grade accessibility".
- `thinking=true` makes LM produce audio codes that DiT consumes (lm-dit); `thinking=false` = pure DiT text2music and any supplied `audio_code_string` is ignored. LM is automatically skipped for cover, repaint, extract even if thinking=true; used only for text2music, lego, complete — `docs/en/API.md` lines 160-169, `docs/en/INFERENCE.md` line 1059.
- VRAM (DOCUMENTED): 2B DiT weights ~4.7 GB, XL 4B DiT ~9 GB (min 12 GB with offload+quant, 20 GB+ recommended); XL not supported at <12 GB. Tier table: 6-8 GB = Tier 3, LM 0.6B only, backend `pt`, CPU+DiT offload, INT8, max duration 8 min with LM / 10 min without, batch 2; 8-12 GB = Tier 4, LM 0.6B, backend vllm, same limits (batch 2 with LM / 4 without) — `docs/en/GPU_COMPATIBILITY.md` lines 7-17; README hardware table: 6-8 GB = 2B turbo + 0.6B (pt); 8-16 GB = 2B turbo/sft + 0.6B/1.7B — `README.md` lines 131-138. Tutorial says "VRAM < 8GB: No LM or 0.6B; 8-16GB: 1.7B" (slightly more permissive than GPU_COMPATIBILITY, which lists 1.7B only from 12 GB) — `docs/en/Tutorial.md` lines 182-185.
- Conflict inside docs: README/Tutorial/source say extract/lego/complete require base (`acestep/constants.py` `TASK_TYPES_TURBO = text2music, repaint, cover, cover-nofsq`; `TASK_TYPES_BASE` adds extract, lego, complete); the Musician's Guide says "base/SFT" (lines 90-133). The README table says sft does NOT support them. Trust source/README.
- DCW (DOCUMENTED, new in repo, CVPR 2026 sampler correction): enabled by default for turbo, off for non-turbo; defaults double/0.05/0.02/haar (0.02/0.06 in Think mode); usable range 0-0.1, higher gives artefacts — `docs/en/DCW.md`. Claimed quality improvement, not a coherence claim.
- Third-party ranking disagreeing with the official table (low reliability, single Japanese blog on XL, ranks turbo above sft/base): [note.com post](https://note.com/vphirosan1128/n/n98e2ae063e43?hl=en). A tech-magazine article claims it beats Suno on coherence; unverified, not primary — [Canadian Technology Magazine](https://canadiantechnologymagazine.com/ace-step-1-5-xl-local-offline-ai-music/).

### Inferences
- For an 8 GB AMD/ROCm card the realistic matrix is: {turbo | sft | base (2B)} x {0.6B LM | no LM}. Turbo is already installed; sft/base need `uv run acestep-download --model acestep-v15-sft` (command from `docs/en/Tutorial.md` line 299 pattern). sft is the most defensible candidate to test for "final" coherence runs (50 steps, CFG, better semantic parsing per docs) but costs ~6x the steps; base is not recommended for plain text2music (rated lowest quality).
- 1.7B LM may fit on 8 GB with offload (Tutorial) but the official tier table allows only 0.6B at <12 GB; treat as untested. The 4B LM's better "Copy Melody"/composition is the documented lever for structure but is out of reach on 8 GB.
- Because LM is skipped for cover/repaint, LM size affects only the first seed generation, not the refinement passes.

### Gaps
- No published A/B of turbo vs sft vs base specifically on long-form structure consistency; no per-variant tempo/key stability data. No HF model card text for turbo-shift variants was retrievable (card page lists only base/sft/turbo/turbo-rl). Actual VRAM of the ROCm path at 8 GB not measured in docs. Full paper (limitations section) not read beyond the summary.

## 2. Reported failure modes and mitigations

### Takeaway
Official docs admit long generations "may have repetition or structure issues" and say short (30-60 s) and medium (2-4 min) songs are stable; they give no failure catalogue. Specific reports on drift/key/tempo are community anecdote and often about ACE-Step v1, so low confidence for 1.5.

### Cited Findings
- DOCUMENTED duration guidance: "Short songs (30–60s) and medium length (2–4min) are stable; very long generation may have repetition or structure issues" — `docs/en/Tutorial.md` line ~676. Instrumental 30-180 s "works well"; with lyrics leave duration auto (-1); long form up to 600 s; 10-20 s minimum — `docs/en/INFERENCE.md` lines 1037-1042. API range 10-600 s — `docs/en/API.md` line 189.
- DOCUMENTED metadata boundaries: BPM 60-180 reliable, extremes unstable; common keys (C, G, D, Am, Em) stable, rare keys "may be ignored or shifted"; 4/4 most reliable; BPM/key are anchors, result may be 118 or 122 for bpm=120 (so tempo wobble is expected behaviour); do not put BPM/key in the caption, use the dedicated params; caption/metadata conflicts (e.g. "slow ballad" with bpm=160) confuse the model — `docs/en/Tutorial.md` lines 656-695.
- DOCUMENTED caption/lyrics consistency: Lyrics tags and caption should not conflict (example note at `docs/en/Tutorial.md` ~line 646).
- DOCUMENTED when LM infers metadata (`use_cot_metas`) BPM/key/duration are filled automatically; user values always win — `docs/en/API.md` lines 171-181.
- DOCUMENTED fallback bug-class: duration<=0 without LM auto-detect used random durations and produced garbage output (issue #929); now falls back to 120 s — comment in `acestep/core/generation/handler/padding_utils.py` lines 78-81. PR #1031 (merged): cover/repaint use src_audio duration instead of the caller's value (referenced from [issue #1116](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5/issues/1116)).
- DOCUMENTED repaint-lyrics pitfall: to change lyrics in a window you must pass the ENTIRE revised lyrics, not only the window's; one reporter still got an instrumental repaint with balanced mode, strength 0.2, thinking off (unresolved in thread) — [issue #1116](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5/issues/1116).
- DOCUMENTED reproducibility advice: fix `seed` when tuning; `lm_temperature` 0.7-0.85 for consistency, 0.9-1.1 for diversity; tuning effects and randomness have "comparable influence" — `docs/en/INFERENCE.md` lines ~1019-1031, `docs/en/Tutorial.md` lines ~855-860.
- ANECDOTE, probably ACE-Step v1 (post dated 2025-06-08, page never states version): long durations lose "initial theme, key, or rhythm" ("attention span"); start at 90-120 s, go to 180 s only after prompts are reliable; shift 6.0 gave more structure and values below 5 were unstable; 65 steps, CFG 4 (6 breaks quality); er_sde + linear_quadratic sampler gave fewer sudden rhythm changes (a later commenter disagreed); expect 3-4 batches (batch 8-16) to find a coherent take — [HF discussion](https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/ACE-Step_ComfyUI_repackaged/discussions/1). These ComfyUI sampler/shift values do not map to the 1.5 repo defaults (API doc: shift 1.0-5.0 default 3.0, steps recommended 32-64 for base) — do not transfer without testing.
- ANECDOTE, unrelated project (not ACE-Step repo): long `[Instrumental]`-only prompts drift off key because there is no section roadmap — [mlip3191/ai_assignment3 #87](https://github.com/mlip3191/ai_assignment3/issues/87); hypothesis only.
- Issue #180 "Song Lyrics Missing in the Song after Generation" exists (not read in detail) — [issue #180](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5/issues/180).

### Inferences
- Main structural mitigation is giving the model an explicit section map: lyrics with `[Intro]/[Verse]/[Chorus]/[Bridge]/[Outro]` tags consistent with the caption, explicit bpm/key/timesignature in params, duration within 2-4 min.
- Tempo/key "wobble" is partly by design (anchors, not constraints); fixing it inside one take is unlikely, so selection (seeds) or conditioning on prior audio (cover/repaint) is the lever.

### Gaps
- No ACE-Step-1.5-specific issue or discussion found on mid-song key change, tempo wobble, off-pitch vocals or repeated/garbled lyrics. The GitHub issue tracker was not searchable with the tools available (only a few issues fetched); Reddit/forum searches returned nothing usable. A fuller issue-tracker pass is recommended.

## 3. Multi-pass workflows and API semantics

### Takeaway
The repo supports: seed batches with automatic scoring (best-of-N), `cover` (structure lock from a source track, strength-controlled), `repaint` (window regeneration; also outpainting past the source end = extend, via padding), `reference_audio` for style/timbre, and base-only `lego`/`complete`. The official docs explicitly advertise repaint chaining for "infinite duration" and stitching.

### Cited Findings
- task_type values: `text2music`, `cover`, `cover-nofsq`, `repaint`, `extract`, `lego`, `complete`; turbo/sft expose text2music, repaint, cover, cover-nofsq; base adds extract, lego, complete — `acestep/constants.py` lines 76-89. Source audio required for cover, cover-nofsq, repaint, lego, extract — `acestep/core/generation/handler/generate_music_request.py` line 155. `flow_edit_morph` is an overlay on cover/cover-nofsq, not a task — `acestep/constants.py` lines 74-76. `cover-nofsq` exists but its semantic difference is not documented in docs (source only maps it to the cover instruction); INFERENCE: probably cover conditioned without FSQ quantization of the source; unverified.
- REST fields (POST release_task): `task_type`, `src_audio_path` or multipart `src_audio`, `reference_audio_path` or `reference_audio`, `repainting_start`, `repainting_end` (-1 = to end), `chunk_mask_mode` (`auto`|`explicit`), `audio_cover_strength` (default 1.0), `cover_noise_strength`, `repaint_mode` (conservative|balanced|aggressive), `repaint_strength` (balanced mode only, 0 = max source preservation, 1 = pure diffusion), `repaint_latent_crossfade_frames` (default 10 = ~0.4 s at 25 Hz), `repaint_wav_crossfade_sec` (default 0 = hard cut), `audio_code_string`, `instruction`, `model` — `docs/en/API.md` lines 246-263, `acestep/api/http/release_task_models.py` lines 40-80, `acestep/inference.py` lines 170-200. Multipart example for repaint at API.md line ~364.
- Cover: keeps melody, rhythm, chords, orchestration and "some timbre"; `audio_cover_strength` higher = closer to source structure, 0.2 recommended for style transfer; lyrics and caption can be changed; multiple generations give variants ("retake lottery") — `docs/en/Tutorial.md` lines 735-775, `docs/en/INFERENCE.md` lines 477-505. Code: when `audio_cover_strength < 1.0` an extra non-cover text conditioning is built to blend — `acestep/core/generation/handler/conditioning_text.py` lines 164-185. `cover_noise_strength`: 0 = pure noise (no cover effect), 1 = closest to source — `acestep/inference.py` line 200.
- Repaint: regenerates window with context; official operating range "3 seconds to 90 seconds"; supports beginning, middle, end, "any region"; changing lyrics/structure, continuing, timbre cloning — `docs/en/Tutorial.md` lines 779-800. Official "infinite duration" recipe: repeated repaint continuations, each 3-90 s, then concatenate; also repaint at the end of one clip, or over the join of two clips, to stitch them — `docs/en/Tutorial.md` lines 802-812. Gradio guide: use repaint to fix sections after generation; "Send To Src" to chain generations — `docs/en/GRADIO_GUIDE.md` lines 503-508.
- Extension mechanics (SOURCE): in repaint/lego, if `repainting_end` exceeds the source duration the source is zero-padded on the right (`right_padding_duration = max(0, actual_end - src_duration)`), and negative `repainting_start` pads on the left; so extend = `task_type=repaint`, `src_audio`=existing track, `repainting_start`=its length (or a bit earlier to overlap), `repainting_end`=target total length — `acestep/core/generation/handler/padding_utils.py` lines 37-68. Not described as "extend" in the API docs; INFERENCE from the code and the Tutorial's "continue writing beginning or ending". Cover does no padding.
- Repaint caveat: repaint also skips the LM, so lyrics/caption for the window come from the request; pass full lyrics (see #1116).
- Reference audio (`reference_audio`) = style/timbre guidance in Custom/text2music; source audio (`src_audio`) = structure for cover/repaint. All DiT variants list "Refer audio" support — README table; `docs/en/Tutorial.md` lines 735-740; `docs/en/GRADIO_GUIDE.md` lines 136, 211-212. The README says the 4B LM has "Strong" melody copy, relevant to audio-code based reference use.
- Lego (base): `instruction="Generate the {TRACK_NAME} track based on the audio context:"`, tracks: vocals, backing_vocals, drums, bass, guitar, keyboard, percussion, strings, synth, fx, brass, woodwinds; Complete (base): `"Complete the input track with {A | B}:"` (adds accompaniment to a single track, e.g. vocals); Extract (base): stem isolation — `docs/en/INFERENCE.md` lines 542-625, `acestep/constants.py` lines 128-138. Note: INFERENCE.md describes `complete` as "complete/extend partial tracks", whereas Tutorial/Gradio describe it as adding accompaniment; the instruction template and Tutorial indicate accompaniment, not temporal extension.
- Best-of-N: batch_size up to 8 (VRAM-limited; 2 on 8 GB with LM), fixed vs random seed, AutoGen background batches, auto-score (perplexity-based) and "DiT Lyrics Alignment Score" ("my favorite", implicitly affects lyric accuracy) for screening, then manual selection — `docs/en/Tutorial.md` lines 928-955, `docs/en/GRADIO_GUIDE.md` lines 330-345. Retake variance (`retake_seed`, `retake_variance`, variance-preserving noise mixing for variations; 0 = no-op) is in `GenerationParams` — `acestep/inference.py` lines 177-180; not described in user docs.
- Repaint tuning (SOURCE): `repaint_mode` conservative = max retention, aggressive = pure diffusion; default balanced/0.5 — `acestep/core/generation/handler/generate_music.py` lines 30-50, `acestep/api/http/release_task_models.py` lines 76-90.
- LM audio codes as style/structure lock: `audio_code_string` (5 Hz codes from an earlier LM run) skips LM generation and conditions DiT; `understand_music` can read codes from audio — `acestep/api/http/release_task_models.py` lines 58-61, `docs/en/INFERENCE.md` line ~625.

### Inferences
- Recommended multi-pass recipe consistent with the docs (untested by me): (1) text2music at 60-120 s (maybe 2-3 min max), explicit section tags + bpm/key/timesig, fixed-seed A/B of a batch of 2-4 seeds on 8 GB, pick by lyrics-alignment score/listening; (2) to lengthen, repaint with `repainting_end` > source length so the model continues from real audio, 30-90 s per step, carrying the same caption/lyrics/bpm/key; keep latent crossfade (default 10 frames) and consider `repaint_wav_crossfade_sec` ~0.1-0.5 s for splices; (3) fix weak sections via repaint with `repaint_mode` balanced/conservative; (4) for style/structure lock across attempts use cover with `audio_cover_strength` 0.7-0.9 (higher = more structure; docs example 0.7-0.8) on the best take, or cover at ~0.2 for restyle only; (5) optional final polish pass with sft + cover at high strength (INFERENCE: cover does not use LM, so sft is the only difference, plausible but undocumented).
- Because cover/repaint conditioning comes from audio, they inherit key/tempo/vocal timbre of the seed, which is the documented mechanism that makes parts consistent; text2music pieces joined by hand have no such link.

### Gaps
- No documented success rate or listening comparison for repaint-extension chains, and no stated limit on how many chained repaints before drift. Exact semantics of `cover-nofsq` and recommended `cover_noise_strength` values not documented. Whether `audio_cover_strength` 0.7-0.9 vs 1.0 changes coherence is not quantified. The extend-by-padding behaviour is from reading source, not from docs, and was not run.

## 4. Short clips + stitching vs one long generation; duration limits

### Takeaway
Official guidance: short and medium songs are the stable regime, very long ones risk repetition/structure issues, and long audio is expected to be built through context-aware repaint continuation (3-90 s windows), not blind concatenation. There is no benchmark proving stitching beats a single 3-4 min generation; the one explicit endorsement is for repaint-based continuation, not naive crossfading of independent clips.

### Cited Findings
- Stable range 30-60 s and 2-4 min; very long may repeat — `docs/en/Tutorial.md` ~line 676. Hard limits: API 10-600 s; tier limits on 6-12 GB GPUs: 8 min with LM, 10 min without LM; ≤4 GB: 4 min / 6 min; LM-constrained duration is clamped to the tier max — `docs/en/GPU_COMPATIBILITY.md` lines 7-17 and notes; `acestep/gpu_config.py` (max_duration_with_lm 480 / without 600 for Tier 2-5).
- Marketing/paper claim: "10 seconds to 10 minutes" supported, scaling "from short loops to 10-minute compositions" — `README.md` lines 43, 56; [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.00744v3).
- Official stitching method: join two audios using repaint at the end of the first or over the seam; model handles rhythm/harmony/timbre connection from context — `docs/en/Tutorial.md` lines 806-812.
- ANECDOTE (v1-era): start at 90-120 s, increase to 180 s only after prompts are reliable; errors compound over long generations — [HF discussion](https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/ACE-Step_ComfyUI_repackaged/discussions/1).
- Speed claim: 240 s track in ~1 s on A100 with 8-step distilled model (paper); sub-10 s per full song on RTX 3090 — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.00744v3), `README.md` line 41. On 8 GB ROCm with offload expect far slower (not measured).

### Inferences
- Best-supported practice: generate at most ~2-4 min in one go (first full-song take), choose the best seed, then extend or repair via repaint/cover rather than concatenating independent text2music clips. Naive concatenation of independent clips lacks shared key/tempo/timbre conditioning and is the scenario the docs' repaint stitch is designed to replace. If a hard crossfade is used, align BPM exactly and cut on bar lines (my inference; not in docs).

### Gaps
- No quantitative evidence on "one long vs stitched" quality; no documented per-section token budget; no 8 GB timing data.

## 5. LoRA / fine-tuning for style consistency (brief)

### Takeaway
Docs position LoRA (and the faster LoKr) as the official route to a consistent personal style/timbre; it needs far more VRAM than inference, so it is not realistic on 8 GB.

### Cited Findings
- README: users can train a LoRA from a few songs; "8 songs, 1 hour on a 3090 (12GB VRAM)" — `README.md` lines 41, 79. LoRA Tutorial states 16 GB minimum, 20 GB+ recommended (full-length songs ~17 GB during training) — `docs/en/LoRA_Training_Tutorial.md` lines 5-8. Musician's Guide: collect 8-20 songs, LoRA scale slider 0-100 %, only one LoRA at a time — `docs/en/ace_step_musicians_guide.md` lines ~470-500.
- LoKr: "up to 10x faster" (5 min instead of an hour), default 500 epochs; community toolkit Side-Step for CLI training and VRAM optimisation — `docs/en/LoRA_Training_Tutorial.md` lines 274-382. Tutorial says LoRA training in the docs targets the turbo model's timestep shift of 3.0 (`training_shift`), and suggests base for large-scale fine-tuning — `docs/en/Tutorial.md` lines 224-234.
- Gradio guide "For Style Consistency": train a LoRA, use reference audio, keep captions consistent — `docs/en/GRADIO_GUIDE.md` lines 510-514.

### Inferences
- LoRA improves consistency of style/timbre across generations but nothing in the docs claims it fixes structural drift within a song.

### Gaps
- No claims or data on LoRA effect on long-form coherence; 8 GB feasibility with Side-Step not checked (its VRAM guide is in `docs/sidestep/VRAM Optimization Guide.md`, not read).
