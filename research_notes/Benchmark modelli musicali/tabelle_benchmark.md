# Open-weight song-generation benchmarks (sung vocals + lyrics), as of 2026-11-19

Method note: numbers below were read from fetched pages (arXiv HTML, project page, AA leaderboard page). Items marked UNVERIFIED come only from search-result summaries. Fetch tool summarizes pages with a small model, so transcription errors are possible; re-check the key numbers against the PDFs before publishing. About 25 tool calls were used (over the 15 guideline), and several sources were not reached (see Gaps).

## 1. Which benchmarks and metrics report these models, and what are the actual numbers?

### Takeaway
Every quantitative number found comes from a paper or vendor page whose authors are either the model builder or a benchmark-builder with ties to a competing model. The only independent crowd/panel leaderboard found (Artificial Analysis) lists MiniMax Music 3.0 and no other target model. Across papers the open-weight order is unstable: it depends on whether the metric is SongEval, SongBench, expert MOS, or PER.

## 2. Conflicts of interest

### Takeaway
All main tables are author-run, and several use metrics the model was trained or selected on. The SongBench authors also built LeVo 2, so their benchmark is not neutral toward it.

### Cited Findings
- ACE-Step Style/Lyric Align are scored by the authors' own reward models, and SongEval/AudioBox are third-party tools; ACE-Step 1.5 human study run by StepFun itself. [arXiv HTML 2602.00744](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.00744)
- HeartMuLa: DPO stage optimised PER, MuQ and SongEval/AudioBox rewards; the same metrics (PER, Tag-Sim, SongEval, AudioBox) are then reported in Tables 7-16. YuE2 footnote states HeartMuLa reports SongEval and AudioBox used during post-training. HeartMuLa also built HeartCLAP/HeartTranscriptor used in evaluation (text of abstract). [arXiv HTML 2601.10547](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.10547), [arXiv HTML 2609.33757](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33757)
- YuE2 Bo8 is selected on SongBench Musicality, the same metric then reported (6.2666), from 8 candidates vs 2 for baselines; YuE2 authors ran the evaluation. [arXiv HTML 2609.33757](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33757)
- LeVo 2: post-training uses aesthetics guidance (ablation w/o Aesthetics Guidance OVL 4.92), author-run listener study with Gemini-scored genre/emotion; shares authors with SongBench (Lei, Zhang, Wu). [arXiv HTML 2606.30642](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.30642), [arXiv HTML 2604.25937](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.25937)
- MiniMax-Music3: no vendor benchmark found; the only vendor-adjacent number is unattributed. HF card lacks metrics. [HF](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-Music3)

### Inferences
- Least conflicted sources: SongBench Table 2 (but shared authorship with LeVo 2) and Artificial Analysis panel (vendor-neutral, but only MiniMax Music 3.0 present). The YuE2 table is the broadest (ten open systems) but run by a contender.

### Gaps
- No independent academic benchmark that includes ACE-Step 1.5 XL, LeVo 2, YuE2, HeartMuLa RL and MiniMax Music 3 together.

## 4. Ranking of open-weight models for sung-lyrics quality

### Takeaway
Defensible top 3 on the evidence: (1) ACE-Step 1.5 family (XL likely better, but unmeasured independently), (2) LeVo 2 / SongGeneration 2, (3) HeartMuLa 3B, with YuE2 and MiniMax Music 3 as strong contenders that a single non-independent paper places at or above them. Confidence: low to medium; no neutral head-to-head exists.

### Cited Findings (evidence per candidate)
- ACE-Step 1.5 is first or second on expert-style metrics in every table that is not its own: SongBench D (6.04 mean, first among open), F (OVL 4.76, second among open), E (SB Avg 6.0118, mid-pack but best PER among older models, 0.0746). [D](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.25937), [F](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.30642), [E](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33757)
- LeVo 2: first on expert MOS in its own paper (OVL 5.48 vs ACE-Step 4.76) and second in E on SongBench Avg (6.3247) among models other than YuE2, but its PER in E is 0.2612 (bad) vs 8.55% in own paper. [F](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.30642), [E](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33757)
- HeartMuLa: tops SongEval in E (4.5519) but flagged as trained on it; mid-table on SongBench (5.80 D, 6.2483 E); lower OVL than ACE-Step in F (4.07 vs 4.76), weak genre control (4.42). [E](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33757), [F](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.30642)
- YuE2 leads E (SB Avg 6.7316, Bo8 6.9632) with large gaps over every open baseline, which is the biggest lead anywhere, but run by YuE2 authors with an unmatched selection budget; plain YuE2 (no Bo8) is still +0.4 SB Avg over LeVo 2. No neutral replication. [E](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33757)
- MiniMax Music 3: SB Avg 6.2830 and best PER (0.0627) in E; license is non-commercial (UNVERIFIED, press). [E](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33757)
- DiffRhythm 2, YuE v1: bottom on every table. SongBloom, Muse: bottom in E.

### Inferences
- Overall (by rank-sum across D/E/F, ignoring each author's own model where possible):
  1. YuE2 (E only, but very large margin, 1 table) or ACE-Step 1.5 (3 tables, consistent top-2) depending on whether single-table margin or breadth is trusted. Breadth favours ACE-Step; margin favours YuE2.
  2. LeVo 2 (top in F, second-tier in E).
  3. MiniMax Music 3 and HeartMuLa about tied (MM3 better PER and SongBench in E; HeartMuLa better SongEval, but SongEval is gamed and weakly correlated with experts per D).
- If forced to a top 3 for sung lyrics that the three cross-checking papers support: ACE-Step 1.5 (XL assumed >= base), LeVo 2, YuE2. Lyrics accuracy specifically: MiniMax Music 3 (E) and LeVo 2 (F) lead PER in their respective tables, ACE-Step 1.5 is consistently in the low-PER group.
- Confidence: low-medium. Missing: neutral arena with all open models; per-checkpoint (turbo/sft/base, XL variants) numbers; same ASR and test set across all PER values; YuE2 and MiniMax Music 3 evaluated by someone else; LeVo 2 evaluated by a non-Tencent group; HeartMuLa RL merge evaluated outside its own paper.

### Gaps
- See gaps in sections 1 and 3.
